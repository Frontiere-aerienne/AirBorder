# Journey preparation source notes

Checked 2026-11-07. These links inform optional preparation guidance, not entry eligibility, live queue estimates, or a guarantee of expedited processing.

- Japan: [Visit Japan Web](https://www.digital.go.jp/en/policies/visit_japan_web) supports online preparation of arrival immigration and customs information. [Japan Customs](https://www.customs.go.jp/english/passenger/egate.htm) describes customs eGates at Haneda and Narita, with inspection still possible.
- UK: [Registered Traveller](https://www.gov.uk/registered-traveller) may help eligible members; [Faster travel through the UK border](https://www.faster-uk-entry.service.gov.uk/about) explains that some passport holders already have eGate access without membership. The app does not recommend enrollment universally.
